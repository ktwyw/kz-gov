Checked in September 2026 against the Prime Minister's official list of state bodies and gov.kz. The structure changed with the 2026 Constitution (in force 1 July 2026): the bicameral Parliament became the unicameral Kurultai, and Constitution Day moved to 15 March. Ministries are reorganised from time to time; gov.kz usually redirects old addresses, but if a link fails, search the body's name on gov.kz. Missing something?
